# Tamil Calendar PWA — Product Overview

## 1. Vision
A lightweight, modern, offline-first Progressive Web App (PWA) displaying the authentic Tamil Calendar (தமிழ் காலண்டர்) for the global Tamil diaspora. The app empowers users worldwide to stay aligned with Tamil time, cosmic rhythms, planetary positions, auspicious times (*Nalla Neram*, *Gowri Panchangam*), and sacred days (*Thithis*, *Vratas*, *Pradosham*, *Sashti*, *Amavasai*, *Pournami*).

> *"வேயுறு தோளிபங்கன் விடமுண்ட கண்டன் மிகநல்ல வீணை தடவி..."*
> — **கோளறு பதிகம் (Kolaru Pathigam)**
> Cosmic bodies, planetary configurations, and gravitational/temporal variations continuously interact with earthly life. This product brings celestial timekeeping into daily modern utility.

---

## 2. Target Audience & Personas
1. **Global Tamil Diaspora**: Tamilians living across different time zones (US, UK, Canada, Singapore, Malaysia, Australia, Europe, India) needing **local coordinates-corrected** Tamil dates and thithis (not just Indian Standard Time reprints).
2. **Everyday Devotees & Families**: Users checking daily *Nalla Neram*, *Rahu Kalam*, *Yamagandam*, fasting days (*Ekadashi*, *Sashti*, *Pradosham*), and auspicious wedding/event dates.
3. **Astrology & Panchangam Enthusiasts**: Users seeking deeper insights into *Nakshatra*, *Yogam*, *Karanam*, *Chandrashtamam*, and planetary transits (*Peyarchi*).

---

## 3. Core Product Principles
* **Zero-Friction Access**: Instant load with no forced sign-in or paywall. The daily calendar is visible on day one in under 1 second.
* **Location-Aware Precision**: Panchangam calculations dynamically adjust based on the user's latitude, longitude, and local sunrise/sunset.
* **Offline-First PWA**: Installable on iOS, Android, and Desktop. Functions seamlessly without internet connectivity using deterministic astronomical calculations.
* **First-Class Bilingualism**: Seamless one-tap toggle between **தமிழ் (Tamil)** and **English** across all labels, months, days, and thithis.
* **Serene, Culturally Resonant UI**: Clean typography, sacred color palettes (deep vermillion, saffron gold, temple bronze, soothing temple dawn tones), and clear visual hierarchy.

---

## 4. Release Roadmap
* **Phase 1 (MVP - Current)**: Daily Calendar view (Moon phase, Tamil Year/Month/Day, Thithi, Nakshatra, Auspicious/Inauspicious timings, Bilingual toggle, Local coordinates calculation).
* **Phase 2**: Monthly grid view with festival markers, search for upcoming festivals/thithis.
* **Phase 3**: Custom notifications/reminders for fasting days (Sashti, Pradosham, Ekadashi) using Local Push Notifications.
